Skills almost every profile asks for

From the SIG's "essential skills for marine mammal professionals".
  • Strong academic grounding, including data analysis
  • Explaining technical issues to specialists and non-specialists
  • Teamwork and adaptability
  • Working to tight timescales in different environments
  • Professionalism and interpersonal skills
  • Project management, organisation and time management
  • Problem solving and intrinsic motivation
  • Attention to detail and safety regulations
  • GIS for mapping data and habitat characterisation
  • R for bioacoustics, visualisation and analysis
  • Python for some bioacoustic tools
  • Fundamentals of statistics
  • Excel and SQLite database experience
Content adapted from the IMarEST MM SIG brochure "Careers with Marine Mammals" (2022, PDF) and role model case studies at imarest.org/rolemodels. Profiles marked Proposed are draft additions for SIG review.
